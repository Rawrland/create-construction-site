# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- **Earthmoving tools:** Excavator Bucket, Bulldozer Blade and Dump Body. They dig, push, carry and tip real blocks, join into bigger tools when placed together, can be painted, and work on Create's contraptions and on Create Aeronautics vehicles.
- **Hydraulic Cylinder:** Cylinder Mounts linked by a Hydraulic Cylinder, with a settings window (decorative or driven, tube length, speed, redstone direction). Driven cylinders push physics vehicles' parts by redstone, with hard end stops (needs Create Aeronautics).
- **Operator Console:** a vehicle control desk with a cross of four buttons, four bonus buttons and five levers, each on its own Redstone Link frequency; taken over with the keyboard or worked by scrolling.
- **Site barriers:** Traffic Cone, Warning Flasher, Warning Beacon, Delineator Post, Barrier Board and Site Fence.
- **Building blocks:** Brass Plating in 16 colours, Dark Steel and Polished Iron Plating, Tread Plate, Grating, Hazard Stripes, Hazard Plate, Corrugated Brass Sheet, several with slabs and stairs.
- Create Aeronautics, Simulated and Sable are optional.
- No recipes yet for the tools, the cylinder, the console and the site barriers: they are creative-only for now.
